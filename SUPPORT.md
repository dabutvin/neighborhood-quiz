## Common questions

**How do I play?**
Each round asks for ten neighborhoods. Tap where you think one is, then tap **Answer**.
You get three tries at each: the first pays $5, the second $3 and the third $1. The
street names on the map are your clues. To see the tips from your first round again,
open **Settings** and tap **How to play**.

**How do I get the other boroughs?**
Manhattan is free. The money you earn in rounds is kept, and it buys the other boroughs
from the **Boroughs** screen, in any order. The first costs $200 of game money, and
each one after costs more. There are no in-app purchases; every borough is earned by
playing.

**Where is my progress saved? Will it move to a new phone?**
On your phone only. There is no account, so your progress does not sync between
devices. If you restore a new phone from a backup of the old one, your progress comes
with it.

**How do I start over?**
Open **Settings** and tap **Delete all saved data**. This erases your money, your
boroughs and your best rounds, and the app goes back to how it was when you first
opened it. It cannot be undone.

**What does the app collect?**
An anonymous count of how rounds go, such as which neighborhoods are hardest to find,
so the game can be tuned. It contains no name, account, email, location or advertising
identifier. To turn it off, open **Settings** and switch off **Anonymous usage**.

**A neighborhood's boundary looks wrong.**
The neighborhoods, streets and parks come from the City of New York's own open data,
so some names and lines may not match what you would call a place. Email with the
neighborhood and what looks off, and it will be looked at.
