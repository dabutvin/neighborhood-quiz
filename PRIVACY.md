# NYC Neighborhoods: Map Quiz — Privacy Policy

*Effective October 4, 2026*

NYC Neighborhoods: Map Quiz is a game about finding New York's neighborhoods on a map.
It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it does not ask for your name,
email, location, contacts, photos or anything else about you.

## What stays on your phone

Your progress — the money you have earned, the boroughs you have bought, your best
rounds, and whether you have seen the tutorial — is saved on your phone and nowhere
else. It is not sent anywhere. It is included in your phone's own backups (iCloud or
your computer), the same as any app's saved data, and comes back if you restore from one.

To erase it, open **Settings** in the app and tap **Delete all saved data**. The app
goes back to how it was when you first opened it.

## What is sent, and why

To learn how the game is played — above all, which neighborhoods are hardest to find —
the app sends an anonymous count of what happens in it to
[TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com), an analytics service built for privacy.
That count is:

- **What happens in the game:** the app being opened; a round starting, finishing or
  being left, with its score; which neighborhood each question was about and on which
  try it was found, or that it was not; a borough being bought, and which borough the
  next round is about; the tutorial tips being shown, skipped, finished or replayed;
  the app asking for a rating; and the Boroughs screen, Settings, the rating page, the
  anonymous-usage switch and **Delete all saved data** being used.
- **About the app and phone:** the app's version, the iOS version, and the phone's
  language and region setting (for example, "en_US").
- **Two random numbers:** one made on your phone the first time the app opens, and
  scrambled (hashed) before it is sent, so the counts from one phone can be told apart
  from another's without saying whose phone it is; and one made fresh each time the app
  is opened.

That is everything. There is nothing in it that identifies you: no name, account,
email, phone number, location, advertising identifier or device identifier, and nothing
you have typed — there is nowhere in the app to type anything. Like any request over
the internet, it reaches TelemetryDeck from your network's address; the app does not
include that address in what it sends. TelemetryDeck's own policy is at
[telemetrydeck.com/privacy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

None of this is used to track you across other apps or websites, to show you ads, or
for anything but improving this game, and it is not sold or shared with anyone else.

## Turning it off

Open **Settings** in the app and switch off **Anonymous usage**. From then on nothing
is counted or sent. The app sends one last count saying it was switched off, so that
people switching it off can be told apart from people who stopped playing.

**Delete all saved data** also forgets the random number made for your phone, so the
app starts over as if it had just been installed.

## Children

The game has no accounts and collects nothing that identifies anyone, of any age.
